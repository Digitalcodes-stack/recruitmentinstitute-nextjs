2. DETAILED ARCHITECTURAL & VOICE SYSTEM INVESTIGATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. Analysis of Why the AI Counsellor Was "Mostly Talking and Not Listening":
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Opening Line Overload:
   - In `gemini_bridge.py`, `_trigger_opening_line()` was transmitting a 3-sentence instruction:
     "नमस्कार! मी रिक्रूटमेंट इन्स्टिट्यूटमधून पूजा बोलत आहे. तुम्ही आमच्या प्रॅक्टिकल
      रिक्रूटमेंट आणि एचआर ट्रेनिंगसाठी कॉल विनंती केली होती, म्हणून मी लगेच कॉल केला आहे.
      कसे आहात तुम्ही?"
   - Because this greeting took 8–10 seconds to generate and speak, it sounded like a
     pre-recorded broadcast rather than a live conversational partner.

2. Lack of Strict Length Guardrails:
   - The original system prompt detailed 7 comprehensive conversational stages
     (Syllabus, Boolean search, ATS, Portals, Placements, FAQs, Slot booking).
   - Without a hard constraint on sentence count, the LLM delivered 4–6 sentence
     mini-lectures every turn, overwhelming the caller.

3. VAD Silence Duration & Sensitivity Lag:
   - `gemini_bridge.py` had configured:
       silenceDurationMs: 1400
       endOfSpeechSensitivity: "END_SENSITIVITY_LOW"
   - Requiring 1.4 seconds of absolute silence before recognizing turn completion
     caused unnatural pauses, sluggishness, and delayed responses.

4. Audio Buffer Barge-in Guard Flaw:
   - When Gemini Live flagged an interruption (`server_content.get("interrupted")`),
     the code previously had a guard: `if self._opening_greeting_delivered:`.
   - Because `_opening_greeting_delivered` was only set upon `turnComplete`, if the
     caller said "Hello? Hello Pooja?" during the opening greeting, barge-in was
     completely suppressed! The bot continued talking over the user.

B. Analysis of Why Language Matching Was Broken:
------------------------------------------------
1. In `prompt_builder.py`, the generated prompt contained the following header:
   "# 🚨 ABSOLUTE HIGHEST PRIORITY DIRECTIVE: MANDATORY LANGUAGE REQUIREMENT (MARATHI)
    - YOU MUST SPEAK EXCLUSIVELY IN MARATHI FROM THE VERY FIRST WORD UNTIL THE CALL ENDS.
    - DO NOT DEFAULT TO ENGLISH. DO NOT INTRODUCE YOURSELF IN ENGLISH.
    - Only switch to English if the caller explicitly instructs you: 'Can you speak in English?'.
    - Otherwise, 100% of your responses must be in Marathi."
2. Result: Even when the candidate greeted or responded in Hindi ("हाँ जी, मुझे कोर्स के बारे में जानना है")
   or in English ("Hello, could you explain the fee structure?"), Gemini was explicitly
   forbidden by the system instruction from switching languages.
