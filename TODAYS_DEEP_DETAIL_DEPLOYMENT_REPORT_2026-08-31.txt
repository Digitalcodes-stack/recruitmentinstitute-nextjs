--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TASK 1: AI VOICE ASSISTANT ("PRIYA") AUDIOCONTEXT SILENCE FIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Problem:
  - When users clicked the "Call" button to initiate a voice conversation with 
    Priya, the WebSocket connected and received audio chunks from Gemini Live, 
    but the browser's Web Audio API AudioContext was suspended due to autoplay 
    policies, causing silent calls.

• Solution Implemented:
  - In public/desk/admin/talk-widget.js:
    1. Ensured AudioContext initialization immediately on the user's click event.
    2. Added explicit `audioCtx.resume()` verification inside the connection flow.
    3. Queued and played incoming PCM audio chunks smoothly with animated avatar 
       mouth frames synchronized to speech volume levels.
  - Verified audio speech plays crisply through laptop/mobile speakers upon clicking 
    [📞 Call].
